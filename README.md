Project Title: "FIN Track: Automated Accounting and Financial Management System" 

Project Description: 

Project being Address: A lot of people and small businesses still track their money using notebooks or scattered spreadsheets. It takes time, mistakes happen easily, and it is hard to see where the money is actually going. This mostly affects individuals who want to keep track of their daily spending, as well as small business owners and bookkeepers who handle sales and expenses regularly. 
It is worth solving because organized records make budgeting and decision-making much easier. FIN Track: Automated Accounting and Financial Management System is a Java-based application that automates recording, categorizing, and summarizing transactions, so users can manage their finances faster and with fewer errors.

Project Objectives: To develop a Java-based application that will automate accounting tasks and manage personal and business finances by recording, organizing, and reporting financial transactions accurately and efficiently.
